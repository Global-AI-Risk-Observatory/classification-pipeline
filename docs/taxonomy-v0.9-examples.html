AI disclosure taxonomy v0.9: eleven fields, one example each

One label per passage. Fields are coded in the order shown; the fields with a blue top edge exist only when the role above them was given. Every quote is a verbatim evidence quote from a filing in the 2025 H1 corpus (US pipeline run us-2025h1_V3), with the filing's form and filing date.

GateIs the passage about AI?

Step 1Which function does the AI sentence have?
frame
Exactly one value, fixed before any role or trait is judged. Order of tests: a possible harm is risk_factor; an oversight remit is governance; demand, prices or supply is market; otherwise business.
